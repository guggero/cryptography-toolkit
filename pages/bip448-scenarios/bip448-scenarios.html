BIP-448: Scenarios

Explanation

BIP 448 proposes to add three op codes to tapscript with one soft fork:
  • OP_TEMPLATEHASH (BIP 446) pushes a hash of the spending transaction that leaves out the outputs being spent.
  • OP_CHECKSIGFROMSTACK (BIP 348) checks a signature over a message from the stack.
  • OP_INTERNALKEY (BIP 349) pushes the taproot internal key.
Each one is small. This page walks through what they make possible when combined, one step at a time. Nothing here is mocked: every transaction is built in your browser and every check mark is the verdict of the script engine of btcd, with the three op codes active. Any spend can be opened in the Tapscript Debugger to watch the stack, or to see what an old node would do with it.

The keys of Alice, Bob and Carol are fixed, public example keys. The op codes are a proposal and not active on any Bitcoin network.
